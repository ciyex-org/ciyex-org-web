Why Ciyex

Technology that serves patients, not profits.

Every feature is designed with one question: does this give clinics and patients better tools than what they have today?

Patient-First Design

Patients own their health data. Secure portal access to records, appointments, messaging, and prescriptions. Software that puts people in control.

Community Health Centers

Purpose-built EHR software for community health centers, free clinics, and rural providers who serve the most underserved populations.

Health Equity

Multi-language, culturally responsive, and designed to remove technology barriers for immigrant, uninsured, and underserved communities.

Privacy & Trust

HIPAA-compliant with enterprise-grade encryption. Every patient deserves the same level of data protection, regardless of which software their clinic uses.

Open Source

AGPL-3.0 licensed. No vendor lock-in, no per-patient fees. Communities can self-host and own their healthcare infrastructure.

Interoperable

FHIR R4 native. Connects with labs, pharmacies, and other systems so patients never have to repeat their story at every visit.

Better Tools

deserves this.

Complete health records software, smart clinical documentation tools, and care coordination workflows. The same quality of technology whether you run a rural clinic or a major hospital.
