Choosing the right Electronic Health Record (EHR) system can shape how efficiently your clinic operates. OpenMRS is widely used in public health programmes and offers strong customisation, making it suitable for organisations with specific workflows. OpenEMR provides a broader feature set, including scheduling, billing, and patient management, making it a practical option for clinics seeking an open-source solution.
