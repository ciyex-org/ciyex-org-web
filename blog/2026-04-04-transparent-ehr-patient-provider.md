For years, the Electronic Health Record (EHR) has been viewed as a "third party" in the exam room — often acting as a digital wall between the clinician and the patient. However, when used strategically, technology doesn't have to be a distraction. In fact, it can be a bridge.

<!-- truncate -->

![Transparent EHR Use](/img/blog/hero-transparent-ehr.svg)

At Ciyex, we believe that transparency is the key to modern healthcare. By shifting from "silent charting" to "collaborative documentation," providers can use the EHR to build deeper trust and improve patient health literacy.

## 1. Breaking the "Digital Wall"

The most common complaint from patients is that providers spend the visit looking at a screen rather than at them. Transparent EHR use changes the physical dynamic of the room.

- **Screen Sharing:** Instead of hiding the monitor, position it so the patient can see it. Use the Ciyex interface to show them their lab trends, imaging, or growth charts.
- **The "Co-Pilot" Approach:** Treat the EHR as a shared resource. When patients see their data in real-time, they feel like active participants in their care plan rather than passive recipients of instructions.

## 2. Collaborative Documentation

"Narrating the chart" is a powerful technique to ensure accuracy and build rapport. As you enter data into Ciyex, speak your notes aloud.

- **Verification:** By saying, "I'm noting here that your joint pain has improved by 40% since your last steroid injection, is that accurate?" you ensure the record is correct.
- **Engagement:** This practice keeps the patient engaged in the conversation and prevents long, awkward silences while you type.

## 3. Improving Health Literacy with Visual Data

Medical jargon can be intimidating. Ciyex transforms complex data into easy-to-understand visuals.

- **Trend Graphs:** Showing a patient a graph of their declining A1c levels over six months is far more impactful than simply stating a number.
- **Visual Aid:** Use the EHR to point out specific markers in their history, helping them visualize their journey toward wellness.

## 4. Real-Time Order Transparency

Trust is built when patients understand the "why" behind a treatment. Using Ciyex to enter orders while the patient is present allows for immediate clarification.

- **Reviewing Medications:** Opening the e-Prescribing module together allows you to discuss potential side effects, dosage schedules, and even cost concerns before the patient leaves the office.
- **Closing the Loop:** When a patient sees the referral or lab order being sent instantly, it provides peace of mind that their care is moving forward without delay.

## 5. Strengthening the Post-Visit Connection

Transparency shouldn't end when the patient walks out the door. The **Ciyex Patient Portal** extends the relationship beyond the clinic walls.

- **Open Notes:** Giving patients easy access to their visit summaries and notes encourages them to stay compliant with treatment plans.
- **Secure Messaging:** A transparent EHR allows for quick follow-up questions, ensuring the patient feels supported even between appointments.
